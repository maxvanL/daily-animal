Daily Animal

A homescreen widget that shows a random animal from iNaturalist.

Download for Android

Latest version

On a computer? Scan this with your phone's camera to open this page there.

First install

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details and then Install anyway. This is normal for apps that aren't on the Play Store.
  4. Long-press your homescreen, choose Widgets, find Daily Animal and drag it onto the screen.

Updating

When a new version is out, the Daily Animal app shows a notice that links here. Download it and install it over the old one. Your widgets and settings are kept.

Prefer automatic updates? Install Obtainium, then add Daily Animal to it (or add github.com/maxvanL/daily-animal by hand).

Animal names and photos come from iNaturalist. Tap a widget to see the animal and its photo credits there.
